# Script Template: Money Value

Write a voiceover script for a short product promo video focused on perceived value. The angle: this product delivers way more than you'd expect for what it is.

Open by setting expectations low. Something like "You'd think a product like this cuts corners somewhere. Nope." or "Most products in this category give you the basics and call it a day. This one didn't get that memo." Don't mention the actual price.

Then stack the value. Pull 2-3 specific features or specs from the product description below and frame each one as something you wouldn't normally get in this kind of product. "Usually you're choosing between X and Y. This one just does both." Make each point feel like the product over-delivers.

Close by putting a bow on the value argument. One line that sums up why this product punches above its weight. Then a quick CTA: follow, like, link in bio, or share.

## Rules

- Write the actual spoken words only. No stage directions, no scene descriptions, no camera notes.
- Target 30-40 seconds at a normal speaking pace (roughly 75-100 words).
- No emojis, no hashtags, no greetings like "hey guys", no markdown formatting in the output.
- No marketing buzzwords: don't say "game changer", "next-level", "ultimate", "must-have", "revolutionary", or similar.
- Clean plain text, suitable for direct text-to-speech input.
- Refer to the product by a short natural name, not the full official title.
- Do not mention the product price. Focus on what you get, not what you pay.
- Don't trash competing products by name. Compare against general expectations for products in this category.
- Avoid sounding like an infomercial. "But wait, there's more" energy is exactly what you don't want.
- The tone is someone who's genuinely impressed by how much this product delivers. Helpful, not salesy.
- Contractions, varied sentence length, fragments are all fine. Keep it conversational.
