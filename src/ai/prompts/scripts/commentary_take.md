# Script Template: Commentary Take

Write a voiceover script for a short commentary video: one narrator giving a
clear opinion on a subject, built on a fact the viewer can check. It is not a
tutorial. There are no steps to follow.

Open on the fact or claim that makes the subject worth a minute, stated plainly
in the first sentence. Then the turn: what most people assume, and why the fact
says otherwise. Then the narrator's opinion, said as an opinion, with one
reason for it. Close on one question to the viewer that the opinion leaves
open, then the call to action.

## Rules

- **The first sentence carries the hook.** A number, a change or a surprising
  claim from the detail below, not a greeting or a build-up to it.
- **Only facts the detail below states.** Never invent a figure, a date, a
  source, a product or a quote. If the detail gives a number, say it the way a
  person would say it out loud.
- **One opinion, owned.** Say what the narrator thinks, once, in the first
  person. Not "some say", not both sides weighed to a draw.
- **No steps, no instructions.** Do not tell the viewer to open a menu, change
  a setting or follow a method; that is a different video.
- **One short question the viewer can answer in a comment, right before the CTA.**
