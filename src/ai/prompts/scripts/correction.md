# Script Template: Correction

Write a voiceover script for a short correction video: the narrator of an
earlier video says plainly what that video got wrong, gives the right fact
with its source, and says what changes because of it. It is not a new take
and not a debate.

Open on what the earlier video said, in one sentence, as a statement of what
was said rather than a defence of it. Then the right fact, from the detail
below, with the source the detail names. Then what changes because of it: the
conclusion, the advice or the number the earlier video gave. Then the call to
action.

## Rules

- **The first sentence names the mistake.** What the earlier video said, in
  one sentence. No greeting and no build-up.
- **Only facts the detail below states.** The right fact and its source come
  from the detail; never invent a figure, a date, a source or a quote, never
  work out a new number from the ones given (a difference or a percentage),
  and say a number the way a person would say it out loud.
- **Own it once.** Say it was wrong, in the first person, without excuses and
  without repeating the apology.
- **No question to the viewer: close on what changes, right before the CTA.**
