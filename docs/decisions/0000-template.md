# NNNN. Title as a short noun phrase

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context and problem

What forces the choice, in two to five sentences. Link the issue.

## Options considered

- **Option A.** One line on what it is and its main cost.
- **Option B.** Same.

## Decision

The option chosen, and the reason in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work it creates.
